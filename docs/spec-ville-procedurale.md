- Only indirect light is modulated; the ambient floor is 40%, and direct sunlight stays separate.
- Zero-area triangles are removed before validating normals on the remaining surfaces.
- Per-model fingerprinted geometry caches allow recooking one new variant without reducing every other model again.
- The current native material array uses 1024-pixel layers with a complete mip chain.
- Procedural materials add filtered solar cells, wood grain, ceramic joints and paving detail.
- Room and cabin glazing combine exterior reflections with virtual room radiance; exterior depth stays opaque.

**Functional detail and art review**

- Entrances use deep reveals, a canopy or porch, a threshold and an opening system appropriate to their role.
- Collective entrances use sliding actuation and presence/read controls; small service openings can use pulls and hinges.
- This milestone models those systems but does not animate doors or enable interior traversal.
- The source records its primary entrance; placement connects the pedestrian access to that point.
- Source zero is the ground datum. Negative footing geometry stays buried instead of lifting the entire building.
- Functional parts retain semantic provenance, and a source with a door but no opening system fails the source audit.
- A separate nine-ray spatial gate checks a 1.1 m wide standing approach at three heights. A valid doorway mesh does not excuse a planter, reveal or rib blocking its approach.
- Façade attachment samples the entrance width and standing height. Greenhouse vestibules extend continuously to the service spine; their clearance is cut from crossing ribs and glazing.
- Mechanical details include inspection covers, captive fasteners, seals, pipe couplings, suspension pivots, wheel pairs and rail bearings.
- `manufacturing.py` authors continuous portal solids, hollow planter mouldings, seating cradles, forged yokes and saddles fitted to the cabin roof.
- `audit_manufacturing.py` welds import seams within 1 micrometre and rejects disconnected, open, inconsistently oriented or collapsed tagged parts before cooking.
- Independent moving or replaceable parts retain intentional gaps; the topology check does not claim every assembly is one solid.
- Shared 0..1 panel UVs carry shallow seals, vents, captive sockets and control markings in generated colour, normal and roughness maps.
- This is deterministic surface-map generation, not a claimed high-poly projection bake.
- Room and cabin glazing use virtual room intersections in the pixel shader, with filtered contents and view-dependent parallax.
- These virtual rooms change radiance only; they do not create collision, exterior depth or interior geometry.
- Shared material maps keep colour, surface slopes and roughness related without baked sunlight.
- Emission uses Exolune's normalized radiance units: trim 0.055 in daylight and 0.30 at night, panel light 0.065 and virtual-room contribution 0.055. Solar illuminance is PI, not a value in lux.
- Capture cameras account for the source entrance datum and pinned terrain sample; an elevated station foundation must not put the review below its floor.
- Near-field review covers thresholds, openings, furniture and transport mechanisms independently from the city-wide route.
- Record each capture's judgement, its remaining defect and the subsequent correction against exact source/bank/shader hashes.
- AAA is the art target; triangle counts and successful automated checks do not establish that target has been met.

**Ground and access**

- `MYPLANETENGINE_CITY_PROBE` exports the existing `ReliefElevation` in a hidden compute pass.
- Its request is `latitude longitude odd-grid-side cell-metres`.
- The output appends `.bin` and includes the planet seed and anchor.
- The city site samples 513 × 513 points at 2 m spacing.
- Paths ramp up to 8 %, then climb by steps up to 30 %. Lots and furniture keep 10 m from water, paths 6 m.
- Every building, plaza, field terrace and transport footing stands on an earthwork pad. See [earthworks](#earthworks).
- A building's pad is its bounding rectangle plus a 1.5 m level apron, with 3 m rounded corners. It is ground, in the terrain's own materials, not a paved apron.
- A path is built, not draped: level across, 0.15 m above the highest graded ground across it, at its station on its centre line (`layout.py`). The relief never shows through it.
- A 0.06 m stone curb runs along every path and common edge; a stone side runs from the curb down 0.35 m into the ground. Across a slope, that side becomes a low retaining wall.
- Steps stand at the same level, level across their width.
- Path and step triangles supply walking height; curbs' steps and sides are structure.
- Segment polygon boundaries before triangulation; split only long edges, with a maximum 4.5 m span.
- Thin stone borders must not trigger uniform four-way triangle subdivision.
- Buildings taller than 6 m use conservative exterior bounding hulls for walking collision.
- Those hulls deliberately do not model interior openings, porches or accessible hangar interiors.
- Small furniture currently has no detailed collision hull.
- Terrain frequency changes can invalidate a cooked site; regenerate the probe and scene.
- Erosion routes a 36 m river through the former site at −11.04, 49.784. The flat review bank of 2026-11-10 uses the anchor −11.04, 49.7776, about 700 m west.
- The engine does not export river water yet. Without a stated water level, the planner sees only the sea.
- The present scene header enforces the planet seed, but does not enforce a complete runtime terrain-shader hash.

**Earthworks**

- The user asked for earthworks in place of stilts and plinths, in the texture of the ground they stand on, harmonious, not cubic, following the ground (2026-11-11).
- A pad is a rounded rectangle at one level (`tools/city/grading.py`).
- Around it, the ground departs from that level by at most its bank slope. A cut rises uphill; a fill falls downhill.
- Earthworks stay local. On gentle ground a bank keeps 1:2; on steeper ground it is twice as steep as the ground, up to 2:1, so it meets the natural ground within about half the pad's depth (`grading.bank`). No earthwork shows more than 24 m beyond its pad.
- A bank no steeper than its ground never meets it: it regrades the whole slope. On 2026-11-11 two towers at the cliff's foot regraded 88 m of the face that way.
- Where a close neighbour stands at another level, a bank steepens as a terrace's retaining bank would, up to 2:1; so does a bank that would run along a neighbour's bank.
- The bank leaves the pad level and reaches its slope over 2 m. Its crest and toe round over 1.5 m of height. No crease marks the pad's edge.
- Beyond the bank, the natural relief is untouched.
- A pad takes the median ground under it, at least 1 m above water.
- Pads apply in plan order. A new pad's level stays within what its bank allows at its distance from each earlier pad: a later bank never enters an earlier level.
- A neighbour may hold a pad at most 5 m from its median ground, 1.5 m for a field terrace; beyond, that orientation fails.
- Order: guideway footings at their datum, the spaceport, plazas, the cable car, buildings, field terraces.
- The engine grades its relief with the same function: `CityGrade.hlsli` for the terrain mesh, pixels, screen relief, vegetation and horizons; `City::Grade` for walking.
- Erosion, the planet map and the city probe read the natural relief (`CITY_NATURAL_RELIEF`).
- `grading.bin` (`CITYPAD2`) carries up to 900 pads, each with its yaw and its own bank slope, and 6,144 index entries, a 32 × 32 cell index over the land grid. A `CITYPAD1` bank loads with one slope for all.
- Validation re-grades the natural relief point by point, as the engine does, and checks that every building's footprint is level on its pad.
- A bank keeps the ground it was cut from: the terrain classifies it at 40 % of its slope, so a 1:2 bank is soil and grass, not scree. Its light takes its whole slope (`CityBank`, `Materials.hlsli`).
- A point is on a bank where its graded elevation stands within the knee of the bank's envelope, less 0.4 m for the pixel's interpolation.
- Steps are the only masonry left.
- The flight contact meets the graded ground too: its constants carry the same earthworks after its probe (`FlightGround.hlsl`). A free camera descends into a cut and stops on a fill.
- The batch cameras' ground (`MYPLANETENGINE_BATCH`) stays the natural relief: a capture states its height above it.

**Runtime**

- `City` loads one shared mesh/material library and a separate placement scene.
- `D3DAppCity.cpp` establishes the double-precision planetary anchor and camera-relative frame.
- Instances batch by model and LOD after CPU frustum and projected-size selection.
- The projected-size thresholds are 520, 260, 115 and 42 pixels with a 20% complementary dither relay.
- Transitions use the existing 64-frame coverage sequence and freeze when temporal antialiasing is disabled.
- D3D vertex pulling offsets the instance SRV explicitly; `SV_InstanceID` starts at zero.
- Buildings share the game's depth, atmosphere, temporal antialiasing and solar-light output.
- A 4096-pixel, 760 m local shadow map follows the camera and illuminates buildings and existing ground consistently.
- Snap the light projection to shadow texels and fade its outer boundary.
- Group shadow casters by model, cull outside the local volume, and omit small distant casters.
- Split manufactured ground into 64 m chunks for frustum culling; walking still uses the full exact surface.
- Architecture shadows currently use geometry level 2.
- Optional resources are initialized even when the district starts outside the visible range.
- The coarsest mesh is the distant representation in this version.
- City impostors, hierarchical proxies, multiple resident districts and planet-wide streaming are not implemented.
- GPU residency, shadow cost and combined vegetation cost require requested measurements before production acceptance.
