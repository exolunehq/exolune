# Vegetation diversity history

**2026-11-02 · Initial fir reduction and witness**
- A first 12,000-triangle reduction left the modeled needle surfaces too sparse in native close views.
- The open fir retained about 39% of its source twig surface area; no performance or quality benchmark was run.
- That prototype increased the budget to 48,000 triangles per fir; it was later superseded by game silhouettes.
- The earlier boreal witness at −49.629°, 90.529° is ocean with the current terrain.
- The first replacement at 42°, 65° is a clearing with conifers only in the distance.
- Close witnesses are centred on the actual exported conifer locations, around 42.00254°, 65.00303°.
- Current rules and validation: [spec-diversite-vegetale.md](../spec-diversite-vegetale.md).

**2026-11-02 · Superseded source-library expansion**
- The first catalogue held 29 models, including two scanned Poly Haven fir saplings.
- The first herb ceiling was 18%, required grassy material and used annual temperature.
- The rooted prototype uses 31 models, game-pine companions, ferns, a 32% herb ceiling and the growing season.
- First-stage command storage was 2,136 bytes; it is 2,280 bytes for 31 models.
- First-stage reports remain in `x64/VegetationDiversity/checks/`.
- Release x64, 31-model asset checks, sixteen hidden renders and 4,000 root-plane cases pass for the rooted prototype.
- Those checks establish technical behavior; the models remain too close to the original libraries and the understory is too dark.
- The user requests a fresh generative foundation; tool comparison is current in `../recherche-generation-vegetale.md`.
