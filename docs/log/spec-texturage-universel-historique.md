# History of terrain material filtering

**2026-11-20 · Triangle slope on the approach**

- A first triangle contribution restored the distant wall but introduced occasional rock streaks on the adjacent hillside. Weighting the measured loss of slope reserves that contribution for places where the centred normal flattens the face.

**2026-11-20 · Close cliff cover trials**

- The former 60 m support could average the exposed wall with its terraces. When a wide tap was culled, the fallback switched to the short normal. The reported straight edge followed the resulting rock mask under one sun and vanished under the second as shadow culling changed.
- A diagnostic radial fallback removed the straight edge by treating the wall as soil throughout. A short baseline everywhere exposed the wall and changed rock coverage on the adjoining hills. Both controls were discarded in favour of the resolved wall's slope transition.

**2026-11-20 · Replaced the extra diffuse tint on mixed cover**

- The earlier palette added up to 28% dust tint when mineral and plantable terrain met. The brightest diffuse scree used 1.04 times the muted lithology, separately from the exposed rock's own albedo.
- The bright line was visible in albedo and under both sun directions. The trace followed scree around interruptions in the canopy.
- The first soil and rock mixture used a constant 58% base fragment share. The revised mixture reduces it at low exposed rock cover. In the 7.2 km witness crop, mean positive band-pass luma at the strongest baseline edges fell from 12.58 to 7.78 and then 5.52 display levels. Under western sunlight it fell from 10.07 to 6.28 and then 4.48.

**2026-11-20 · Replaced the intermediate cover handoff**

- The wide cover normal admitted the fine grid only as its own cells grew from 30 to 60 m. Fine cells between 14 and 23 m supplied terrain light during the reported approach while the 48 to 80 m mesh controlled most of the cover.
- The unresolved variance followed the finer water reconstruction's effective cell. Resizing the face shifted rock and forest boundaries at a fixed view.

**2026-11-20 · Palette and cover trials**

- First colour passes faded the diffuse palette over 4 to 32 m of pixel footprint, then 1 to 8 m. The hue returned toward its previous value during approach. The current palette applies at every scale.
- The hillside's water view showed a river at the left and dry ground on the reported slope. The albedo already showed the moving boundaries when the mesh was resized at the same camera placement.
- Removing lighting grain from the fine-grid cover calculation reduced colour reversals. This trial still showed fine ribbons when the grid cell changed. The wide physical support replaced that decision scale.

**Earlier fine-grid threshold, 2026-10-24**

- The fine-grid normal originally faded from 20 m cells. Snow and rock shapes at wavelengths of 13.5 to 100 m then came and went between 2 km and 160 m. Extending the fine grid restored those shapes before the cover and lighting scales were separated. See `../journal-planete.md`.
